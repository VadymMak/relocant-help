About Anna

I help Ukrainian and Russian-speaking professionals relocate to Germany and build a stable legal foundation here. After nine years of practice — six in Berlin — I've handled over 400 successful Blue Card and family reunion cases.

My focus is on tech professionals, freelancers, and families with children. I work directly with the Ausländerbehörde in Berlin, Brandenburg, and Saxony, and I know the realistic timelines for each office. No promises I can't keep.

Consultations happen on Zoom or at my Mitte office. Documents reviewed in advance — you walk out with a clear plan, not more questions.
